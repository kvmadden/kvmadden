### Kevin Madden, PharmD

Pharmacist and healthcare leader who designs AI tools for clinical work and tests where they fail.

My independent projects focus on practical workflows, measurable results, and clearly stated limitations.

**Selected work**

- [Clinical evidence map](https://kvmadden.com/projects/clinical-evidence-map/): a review desk for faxed prior-authorization packets. In one test with synthetic packets, the routing system handled 73 of 120 calls without a reviewer, with zero silent errors against draft labels.
- [Redaction gate](https://kvmadden.com/projects/pii-redaction-gate/): strips identifiers from clinical text before it reaches a model. On 132 synthetic documents, it sent 120 for review and left 12 of 2,577 identifiers in documents it marked clean.
- [jargon](https://kvmadden.com/projects/jargon/): a learning app whose AI checker I tested as a pharmacist. At least 9 of 78 accepted explanations contained an error. As a pharmacist, I confirmed five of those errors. [Try it →](https://jargon.kvmadden.com/)
- [knowledge-mcp](https://kvmadden.com/projects/knowledge-mcp/): agent memory with one screening gate on every write through its MCP tools. None of 324 planted values reached storage in a test of how write tools reach the gate.
- [Counter Intelligence](https://kvmadden.com/projects/counter-intelligence-sim/): a branching game for practicing vaccine conversations. All 24 clinical claims cite a public-health source.
- [Vax or Fiction](https://kvmadden.com/projects/vax-or-fiction-game/): a room game for talking through immunizations. All 144 questions show a public source. Clinical review is pending. [Try it →](https://vaxorfiction.com/)
